Before You Begin

This is a demonstration for the H04 HTML learning challenge, not a real application service or an announcement of training vacancies. Use sample information only. Applications are not submitted, and AboudTech does not receive or store the information entered here.

Choose "Check application (demo only)" to use your browser's validation. If a field is invalid, the browser will identify a problem. When all fields are valid, this page's submission policy blocks the request. The form stays here without a confirmation message, and no entered values are added to the URL or sent to a service. Use a current browser with Content Security Policy support for this demo.

A backend will be needed to receive, securely validate, store, and process real applications. This page cannot check whether contact details or profile links belong to a real person.

  • Fields marked "required" must be completed.
  • GitHub, portfolio, and programming language selections are optional.
  • Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move between controls.
  • Use arrow keys for radio choices and Space to toggle checkboxes.
  • Date and year limits are fixed demo examples, not eligibility rules.

Student Application Form

Personal Details

Enter 7 to 25 characters. Spaces and a country code may be included. This field checks length, not whether a phone number is valid.

Demo range: January 1, 1900 to November 14, 2026.

Education

Enter a whole year from 2026 to 2100 for this demo.

Training Preferences

Choose November 14, 2026 or later for this demo. These fixed dates do not update automatically.

Current Programming Experience (required)

Choose the description that best matches your current experience.

Programming Languages (optional)

Select all languages you have used, or leave this group blank.

Online Profiles (optional)

Enter complete URLs, including https://. Leave a field blank if you do not have that profile. URL format is checked, but links are not opened.

Motivation and Agreement

Use 50 to 2,000 characters to describe what you would like to learn and why this training interests you. Use sample content for the demo.
